# IdleTimeoutDialog

Warns before a session ends after inactivity and lets the person continue with one tap.

Defaults: times out after 20 minutes, warns 2 minutes before (WCAG 2.2.1 needs at least 20 seconds). The remaining time is shown in words and announced once a minute, not every second. Keep what the person typed when it times out; older users are often slow, interrupted, or helped by a relative. Disable it on finished or empty pages.
