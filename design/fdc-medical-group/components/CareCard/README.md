# CareCard

"When to get help" advice in three levels: non-urgent (group blue), urgent (red), emergency (red heading, dark body, "Gọi 115 ngay").

The level is in the heading words for screen readers ("Cấp cứu: …") and in the band color, never color alone. Non-urgent stays FDC blue in both themes so it is never confused with urgent red. Order cards from least to most serious, keep lists short and specific, and have a doctor approve the content. Pink is never used here: it is the hotline color.
