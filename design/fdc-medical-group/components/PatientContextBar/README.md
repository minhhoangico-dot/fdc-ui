# PatientContextBar

Who is being worked on, always in view on a staff form: the name as the page heading (`headingLevel`, default 1) with the record's state right after it (a StatusTag), then the facts that tell people apart, and the way to the previous or next person. A Breadcrumb sits above the name.

**Rows**: the breadcrumb; the name and its state, with the previous/next buttons at the end of the same row from 40rem up; the facts. So from 1024px, where the bar sticks, it is about 120px tall at every width and never grows a row of buttons under the facts (version 2.4; it was 185px at 1024px). When the row is too narrow the state wraps under the name.

**Facts** (version 2.4) are whole chunks set apart by space, never by "·" (a dot left at a line end reads like a stray mark): sex unlabelled ("Nữ"), "Sinh 07/07/1990 (36 tuổi)", "CCCD …0006" (last four digits only), "Mã lượt khám **RD26-0006**" (`recordCodeLabel`, the code bold as the value), then the company or ward unlabelled. Each chunk stays on one line; only the company wraps inside itself.

**Name, date of birth and record code are never hidden**: they are what tells two people with similar names apart. Missing facts are simply left out (no "undefined", no empty gaps). The age is counted on `ageOn` (set it to the exam date for past records).

**Previous and next** (`actions`): two Buttons with short visible words, "Người trước" with its arrow before the words and "Người sau" with its arrow after them (`iconPosition="end"`). Each accessible name starts with the visible words and adds the person: `aria-label="Người trước: Ngô Văn Nam"`, `aria-label="Người sau: Nguyễn Thị Mai Anh"`.

**Phones** (below 40rem): the name is 1.25rem (1.5rem from 40rem up), the spacing is tighter, and the two buttons share one row in two equal columns, each on one line at 44px even at 320px. The bar is as short as it can be without losing a fact, so the first field of a form starts higher: the state sits beside the name whenever both fit; the facts are 10px apart and the code shows without its words ("**RD26-0006**", as in the Header's thin bar; screen readers still hear "Mã lượt khám"), so they take two lines at 390px and three at 320px (version 2.4: the bar is 201px at 390px and 223px at 320px, was 223 and 246).

With `sticky` it stays at the top while the page scrolls, from 1024px up (under any header that sticks above it), in either variant. Below that, the compact Header's thin bar (`collapseOnScroll` + `compactBar`) keeps the name, date of birth and code in view instead, so only one bar holds the top of the screen at any width. On a record form place it in StaffLayout's `context` slot, which measures it and keeps focused fields and the side column clear of it.

**`variant="block"`** is the person among the page's own blocks, for a page whose title comes first, such as the review page ("Xem lại trước khi gửi lên Sở Y tế", then the person with `headingLevel={2}`): a white block with one light edge all round, like the blocks under it. Put no box around it, so there is one edge, not two. `recordFacts` adds a muted line about this version of the record under the facts (exam time, signer, last save), each a whole chunk. With `sticky` and as a direct child of the page's content (StaffLayout's main), it stays at the top from 1024px all the way down to the tick box and the send button (100px tall at 1024px); StaffLayout measures it like the bar, so a focused control never lands under it. On phones the breadcrumb collapses to "‹ parent".
