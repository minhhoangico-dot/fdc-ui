# ServiceCard

A centered card for one service line: illustration, `brand-600` title, short excerpt and an optional "Xem chi tiết" link.

Use in a 3-column grid on a `brand-200` or `surface-alt` section. Pass `image` from Assets › Services (66px illustrations), or `icon` when there is none. Keep excerpts to about 30 words; the card clamps at four lines. With `href` the whole card is a link and lifts with `shadow-card` on hover. Note: the service illustrations have FDC blue baked in, so they do not change color in the nursing home theme.
