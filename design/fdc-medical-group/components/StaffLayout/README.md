# StaffLayout

Page shell for staff tools (hoso, FDC Manager, reception). It sets `data-density="compact"` and lays out:

1. a skip link and the one-row Header (`variant="compact"` is set for you);
2. `context`: a full-width bar under the header, usually a sticky PatientContextBar on a record form;
3. `aside`: the `<nav>` landmark ("Các phần của phiếu") beside the content from 1024px up, above it on smaller screens. Put a **FormPartNav** in it: the full list of form parts with "còn n" counts on computers, one "4. Thể lực · còn 2 ▾" toggle on phones, so staff do not scroll past seven rows to reach the form;
4. `<main id="main">` on the grey work surface (`surface-alt`): blocks inside are white with a 1px edge and spacing, not shadows;
5. `actionBar`: a sticky bottom bar for the save line (SaveStatus) and the screen's buttons. It floats over content, so it is the one block with `shadow-card`. `shortcuts` adds the Kbd hint line under it from 1024px up only: phones have no Ctrl key.

On phones (below 640px) the rhythm is tighter: 10px above the content, 10px under the aside and 15px between blocks (20px from 640px up), so the work area starts sooner. Leave `theme` out to inherit the brand theme from the page; a nursing-home staff tool is orange and compact. One primary button per screen state, and it changes with the moment: unsaved → "Lưu"; saved but items missing → "Đến mục còn thiếu"; complete → "Xem lại trước khi gửi".

**Focused fields are never hidden under a sticky bar** (WCAG 2.4.11). The bars' heights change with the width and the state (the header's thin bar below 1024px, the PatientContextBar from 1024px, an action bar that grows to several lines in a save conflict on a phone), so StaffLayout measures them (ResizeObserver) and writes `--header-space`, `--context-bar-space` and `--action-bar-space` on its root and on `<html>`. The person's bar counts wherever it sticks: in the `context` slot (a record form) or as a PatientContextBar block that sticks inside the content (the review page). The scroll padding is those heights plus a gap: 80px at the top, so a field's label and the message line above its box show too, even when that line takes two lines on a phone, and 15px at the bottom (also with no action bar, for the focus ring). The side column sticks 20px under the measured bars and scrolls inside itself on a short screen. After focus moves by keyboard, a control that the browser left partly under a bar (a textarea scrolls only to its caret; a radio's small input scrolls, not the option that draws its ring) is brought fully into view with its focus ring, measured on the element that draws it (the option, slot or box, plus the ring's width and offset), and so is its label if a taller message line left it under a top bar. On a screen too short for the 80px and 15px (a phone held sideways, 568x320), it falls back to the real bar edges: the label 5px under the top bars and the ring above the action bar, as far up to 15px clear of it as room allows; only a control taller than the whole clear area is left to the browser. Pages never hard-code sticky offsets.
