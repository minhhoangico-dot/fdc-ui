# NewsCard

An article teaser: image, date, title and excerpt.

The image keeps the nursing home's portrait 255:278 ratio and gets the leaf corner (`radius-leaf`) in the nursing-home theme, crisp 3px corners in the clinic theme. Without `image` a `brand-100` placeholder is shown. Pass `date` pre-formatted (dd/mm/yyyy). Titles in sentence case; the excerpt clamps at three lines.
