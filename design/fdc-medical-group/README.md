# FDC Medical Group

One visual language for every screen the group ships: the public clinic website, staff tools like FDC Manager, patient touchpoints, and the nursing home. Everything here was extracted from the live sites bacsigiadinhhanoi.vn (clinic) and fdcmedical.vn (nursing home), so a rebuild matches what patients and families already recognize.

## How the system is organized

The system follows atomic design. Each component has one clear job, reuses whatever existing parts fit (an organism may combine molecules, atoms and other organisms), never depends on a component that depends back on it, and never sets a design value outside the tokens. Do not invent in-between components just to fill a level.

- **Tokens** (colors, type, spacing, radius, shadow) are the single source of truth, in two tiers: the **palette** (`brand-700`, `ink-soft`, `info-600`…) and **roles** that point at it (`text-link`, `surface-selected`, `action-secondary-edge`, `status-info-bg`…). **Components use roles only, never the palette**: `brand-700` is the color, `text-link` is the role, the component uses `text-link`. Then moving the link color cannot repaint a button or a heading by accident. A script fails the build if a component names a palette token.
- **Layout** owns spacing between things: Stack, Inline, FieldRow (fields side by side, labels and inputs aligned). No component sets its own outer margin.
- **Atoms** use only tokens: Button, TextField, Select, Checkbox, Icon, Avatar, TextLink, SkipLink, StatusTag, Kbd.
- **Molecules** combine atoms into one-job blocks: DateInput, PhoneInput, TimeSlotPicker, RadioGroup, SummaryList, CareCard, ConfirmationPanel, HotlineCard, WorkingHours, ServiceCard, SpecialtyTile, ValueCard, TestimonialCard, NewsCard, ServiceAccordion, FormNotice; for staff tools NumberField (a measurement with its unit), Combobox (choose a coded item by name or code), CodeList (several coded items, e.g. diagnoses), FormPartNav (the parts of a long form), SaveStatus, Pagination, Breadcrumb.
- **Organisms** are distinct sections: Header (with a `compact` variant for staff tools), Footer, Dialog, IdleTimeoutDialog, ErrorSummary, ResultLookupForm, ContactForm; for staff tools DataTable (a list of records with paging) and PatientContextBar (who is being worked on).
- **Templates** lay out a page with slots: SiteLayout (skip link, header, main, footer), BookingStep (one question per page) and StaffLayout (compact header, context bar, side column, sticky action bar). The booking flow is: service → who it is for → time → details → check answers (SummaryList) → confirmation (ConfirmationPanel), with IdleTimeoutDialog guarding the session.

The group is modeled as **themes**. `FDC Clinic` (blue) is the default. `FDC Nursing Home` (orange) overrides only the brand scale (`brand-50` to `brand-800`) and `leaf-500`; every other token and every component is shared. Set `data-theme="nursing-home"` on the page root to switch. Never hard-code blue or orange in a component: use a role token (most roles point at `brand-*`, so they follow the theme). `group-accent` (the group blue) and the status colors (`status-info-*`, `status-warning-*`, `status-success-text`, `status-error-text`) stay the same in every theme, so a state means the same thing in every app.

**Density** is a second, independent switch: `data-density="comfortable"` (default; patients and families: 18px text, 48px controls, content width limited) or `data-density="compact"` (staff tools: 16px text via `font-size-body`, 44px controls, wide layouts allowed). Brand and density combine freely: a nursing-home staff tool is orange and compact.

## Content fundamentals

- Write in Vietnamese first, plain and calm. Patients and families read this, often when worried.
- Name things by what the patient does: "Đặt lịch khám", not "Submit".
- Always show the hotline where a patient or family might get stuck: clinic 024 35 430 430, nursing home 096 583 7777.
- Button labels are sentence case, verb first ("Đặt lịch khám"). The site's uppercase labels were dropped: capitals are slower to read and crowd Vietnamese tone marks.
- Vietnamese diacritics need room: body line-height 1.5 or more, headings 1.3 or more.
- Errors say what to do, in the field's own words: "Nhập số điện thoại gồm 10 chữ số, ví dụ 0912 345 678". Never "Sai" or "Không hợp lệ".
- Ask one question per page in booking flows, and ask early who the appointment is for ("Bạn đặt lịch cho ai?"): families often book for parents.
- Before anything is booked, show every answer with a "Thay đổi" link; after, say what happens next, the booking code, and how to change or cancel by phone.
- Health advice uses three levels (CareCard): non-urgent "Gọi bác sĩ gia đình" (FDC blue), urgent "Đến khám trong ngày" (red), emergency "Gọi cấp cứu 115 ngay" (red and dark). Inform and reassure; a doctor approves the wording.
- Write the address, hotline and email as text in the footer, not only as links.

## Công cụ nhân viên (staff tools)

Staff tools (FDC Manager, hoso, reception) are used all day by trained people, often against the clock. They follow the rules above plus these, approved by Minh on 25/10/2026 from the hoso health-record app.

- **One primary button per screen state.** Secondary buttons are white with a brand edge and brand text (`surface`, `action-secondary-edge`, `action-secondary-fg`), so they never look disabled. A button that cannot be used has no fill and a dashed edge, and always has a line right next to it that says why (Button `disabledReason`).
- **Labels say exactly what will happen**, and change with the choice when needed: "Dùng người và lượt khám này", "Nhờ Minh kiểm tra". Never a generic "OK" or "Tiếp".
- **An empty field is neutral, not an error.** A measurement (NumberField) says everything in one message line above the box: "Chưa điền · ví dụ 78" while empty, "ví dụ 78" while typing, nothing once filled and left, the amber "Cao · bình thường 60–100" when outside the normal range, and a red "! Điền mạch, ví dụ 78" for an error. The line keeps its room (TextField `reserve`), so the tag never takes the box's width and boxes in a column keep one right edge. A group of choices shows no "Chưa chọn" hint (an empty group already shows it) and reports its error once for the whole group: one message and one red bar, not a red box on every option. Show red only when a typed value is wrong, or after the user presses check or review and something is still missing.
- **Nothing moves while the person works.** Field errors and the error summary are set when the person presses "Xem lại" or "Lưu", and stay as they are while the person types or leaves fields, until the next press (as on GOV.UK). Nothing above or below a field moves when it is focused, typed into or left, so a click on the next control is never lost.
- **Two different counts, two different names:** "mục còn thiếu" (things still to do) and "ô chưa lưu" (changes not yet saved). Never mix them in one number.
- **Name first, code after in brackets and lighter:** "Tăng huyết áp (I10)", "Nhà chuyên môn về sức khỏe (22)". A code never stands alone. In a field too narrow for both, the name is cut with "…" and the code stays whole: "Nhà chuy… (22)" (Combobox). Codes never break across lines. Codes are set in Be Vietnam Pro, not a monospace face: capital I is a plain bar and 1 has a flag and is half as wide again, and an ICD-10 code is always a letter then digits after its name, so I10 is never read as 110.
- **Status tags are tinted words, never outlined boxes.** A StatusTag has a pale fill and a small dot, so it never looks like a button next to real buttons. None is solid (the `strong` tone is deprecated and drawn as neutral). A note about who does what is plain muted text, not a tag.
- **Say who sends.** A batch is sent by hand or automatically, and the screen says which: the list's plain line "Đợt này gửi tay: chị xem lại rồi tự gửi từng hồ sơ.", repeated where the send happens; the header's auto-send tag names the one batch it runs for ("Gửi tự động: chỉ đợt F.D.I KSK 2026", tone `info`, because running is not success).
- **A value outside the normal range is not an input error.** It gets the `attention` tag ("Cao", "Thấp": amber, `status-warning`); red is kept for values that are wrong or missing. The ranges and whether a very abnormal value needs a confirmation step are set by a doctor, not by the interface.
- **Green is only for success.** Work that was started but is not finished ("Chờ gửi lên Sở", còn 27 giây để hủy) uses `status-info` (pale blue, the same in both themes). Warnings use `status-warning` (amber, the same in both themes, never the nursing-home orange).
- **Bold only emphasizes a value.** Anything that needs attention gets a word label (StatusTag: "Cao", "Nhập tay"); bold or color is never the only signal.
- **No single-letter shortcuts:** with Telex Vietnamese typing they fire by accident. Use Ctrl combinations only, and show the hint where it applies with Kbd ("Ctrl + S lưu").
- **One-row header** (Header `compact`) to leave height for the work. On phones it shrinks while scrolling but always keeps the current person's name, date of birth and record code in view: the thin bar shows the name on one line and the date of birth and code as whole chunks, and is never taller than the full row.
- **Long lists page clearly:** "Hiển thị 1–50 trong 2.033 · ‹ Trước · Sau ›".
- **Filters are pills, questions are tiles.** A list filter is a RadioGroup with `variant="pills"` and `layout="wrap"` ("Cần điền hoặc sửa · 1.019", "Tất cả · 2.033"), so it never looks like a question on a form; on phones it collapses into a Select.
- **The error summary stays small on staff forms.** ErrorSummary `layout="inline"`: a short heading that gives the count ("Còn 4 mục chưa điền") and the fields' short names as links ("Mạch", "Tâm thu", "Tâm trương", "Phân loại thể lực"), side by side in rows that wrap, set apart by space (never by dots), each a 44px target; on a 320px phone four names take two rows. The fields keep their own message ("Điền mạch, ví dụ 78"). It is read once: an alert when it appears, then a group named by its heading, so filling fields and pressing "Lưu" do not read it again. Patient forms keep one problem per line.
- **Compact density** (`data-density="compact"`) on staff tools; separate blocks with background, edges and spacing, not `shadow-card` (keep shadows for things that float: menus, dialogs).
- **Three sample staff screens** show the rules together (Storybook › Pages › Nhân viên, with mock data): the record list (DataTable, 50 a page, filter by group, search without tone marks), result entry (PatientContextBar, FormPartNav, NumberField, CodeList, SaveStatus, the main button changing with the moment) and review before sending (SummaryList with details, the undo window, a refusal, an unknown outcome). Each has a phone view and its failure states: no network, nothing found, a failed save, a refused send.
- **The main button follows the moment:** unsaved changes → "Lưu" (after a failed save, "Lưu lại"); saved but items missing → "Đến mục còn thiếu"; complete → "Xem lại trước khi gửi". Never let the button someone keeps pressing turn into a real send. The send button names who is sent: "Gửi lên Sở: Ngô Văn Nam".
- **One save line tells the truth.** SaveStatus shows exactly one of: unsaved (n ô chưa lưu), saving, saved, failed, or conflict ("Cần xử lý 1 giá trị trước khi lưu", when someone else saved the same field). It never shows "Đã lưu" while a conflict or unsaved change is open. The failed and conflict lines carry the "!" sign. A failed save is read once, at once: an alert holding "Lưu không được: mất mạng. Chữ đã gõ vẫn còn"; the count and "Lưu lại" sit outside it. In a conflict the field is read-only until a value is chosen, and Ctrl + S moves focus to the question, never to one of the answers.
- **Sending never blocks the next person.** During the undo window (panel "Chờ gửi lên Sở", with "Còn 30 giây để hủy." under the title; the heading never counts, so its name stays the same while it has focus) staff move on to the next person. The record waiting to be sent stays findable: "Hủy gửi" at the top of the next page, and in the list (group Đang gửi, "Hủy được đến 09:31:22"), where opening it offers "Hủy gửi". A screen reader hears the moments only: the start, the move to "Đang gửi", a cancel.
- **Say where the answer will land.** While a record is being sent: accepted → group "Sở đã tiếp nhận"; refused → "Cần điền hoặc sửa", with the department's reason and the path sửa → lưu → xem lại → gửi lại; unknown → stays in "Đang gửi"; open the record to check the status.
- **Never offer "Gửi lại" when the outcome is unknown.** If the department did not answer, the department may still have received it: check the status first or ask Minh. Only a refusal with a reason opens the record for a fix and a resend.
- **Several diagnoses, and the doctor's own words.** Diagnoses are a list (CodeList: name first, code after, add and remove), next to a free-text field for the wording on the paper form; not every remark has to become an ICD code.
- **On phones the work comes first.** The parts of a long form sit in StaffLayout's aside as FormPartNav: the full list from 1024px, one "4. Thể lực · còn 2" toggle on phones; lists show each record as a compact block (DataTable `stackBelow="phone"`: name first, one labelled line per column, nothing scrolls sideways) with search before filters. Facts that tell people apart are set apart by space, not by "·".
- **Edges are light, emphasis is rare.** Blocks are separated by a pale `border` edge and space; the strong `border-control` edge is for inputs and the chosen option; bold is for names, values and the main action.
- **One bar holds the top of the screen:** from 1024px up a sticky PatientContextBar; below it the compact Header's thin bar with name, date of birth and code. Focused fields are never hidden under a sticky bar: StaffLayout measures the bars that stick and sets the scroll padding from their real heights (`--header-space`, `--context-bar-space`, `--action-bar-space`); pages never hard-code sticky offsets.
- **Lists and searches:** search accepts text without tone marks ("ton nu" finds "Tôn Nữ"); an empty result says why and how to get back; a failed load says what failed and keeps the filters. A list says its result in one polite live region (count, empty text or failure, on every change); after "Tải lại" focus moves to the first record. Columns keep their widths when a filter changes, the state tag stays on one line with its count under it ("còn 61 mục"), and metadata under a value (date of birth, CCCD …0001, Mã Sở …118204) uses the caption size.
- **Design notes and implementation rules live outside the interface.** On screen, keep only the sentence that helps the user act now.

### Trạng thái nghiệp vụ (record states)

Each state is one real event. Never show a later state because an earlier one succeeded: "Đã gửi" does not mean "Sở đã tiếp nhận"; only the department's receipt does. Version 2.4 split the old "Gửi lỗi" into "Bị từ chối" and "Chưa rõ kết quả", because resending a record the department may already hold creates a duplicate.

| State | StatusTag tone | Means (the event that sets it) | Allowed actions | Next |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chưa điền | muted | Record created, nothing typed | Điền | Đang điền |
| Còn thiếu (n mục) | neutral | Saved, n required items empty | Điền tiếp | Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Cần điền (còn n mục) | neutral | The list's word for Chưa điền and Còn thiếu: required items still empty. The count sits under the tag ("còn 61 mục"), not in it | Điền, Điền tiếp | Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Đang chờ | info | Waiting for someone else: HIS (lab results not approved yet) or Minh (asked to check, e.g. choose the HIS person) | none in the list; Hủy yêu cầu for a request to Minh | Cần điền / Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Ô chưa lưu (n ô) | info | Changes typed, not written yet | Lưu (Ctrl + S), Bỏ thay đổi | Đang lưu |
| Cần xử lý (n giá trị) | attention | Someone else saved the same field after this page was opened | Choose one value ("Dùng 53 kg của chị Mai" / "Giữ 52,5 kg của tôi") | Ô chưa lưu |
| Đang lưu | info | Save request sent | none | Đã lưu / Lưu lỗi |
| Đã lưu | success | Server confirmed the write | Sửa, Xem lại | — |
| Lưu lỗi | error | Server refused or network failed | Lưu lại | Đang lưu |
| Cần sửa | error | Check found a wrong value | Sửa | Còn thiếu / Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Sẵn sàng xem lại | neutral | Saved, nothing missing, no errors | Xem lại trước khi gửi (Ctrl + Enter) | Chờ gửi |
| Chờ gửi (còn n giây để hủy) | info | User confirmed; the undo window runs (staff may already work on the next person) | Hủy gửi | Đang gửi / Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Đang gửi | info | Sent to the department, no answer yet | none | Sở đã tiếp nhận / Bị từ chối / Chưa rõ kết quả |
| Sở đã tiếp nhận | success | Department returned a receipt code | Xem mã | Bộ đã xác nhận |
| Bộ đã xác nhận | success | Ministry confirmed receipt | Xem mã | — |
| Bị từ chối | error | Department refused and gave a reason | Mở phần cần sửa, Nhờ Minh kiểm tra | Cần sửa → Sẵn sàng xem lại |
| Chưa rõ kết quả | attention | No answer in time, or the connection broke after sending: the department may have received it | Kiểm tra lại trạng thái, Nhờ Minh kiểm tra. **Never "Gửi lại"** until the status is known | Sở đã tiếp nhận / Bị từ chối |
| Chờ Minh kiểm tra | info | Staff asked for help (shown in the list as Đang chờ) | Hủy yêu cầu | per Minh's decision |

List groups: Cần điền hoặc sửa = Cần điền, Cần sửa, Bị từ chối; Sẵn sàng xem lại; Đang chờ; Đang gửi = Chờ gửi, Đang gửi, Chưa rõ kết quả; Sở đã tiếp nhận = Sở đã tiếp nhận, Bộ đã xác nhận; Tất cả. "Làm được ngay" counts Cần điền hoặc sửa, Sẵn sàng xem lại and Chưa rõ kết quả.

ConfirmationPanel `steps` take an explicit state per step (`{ label, state }`), so the step line shows only what has actually happened. Done steps carry a ✓ and say what happened ("Đã gửi lên Sở", "Sở đã tiếp nhận"). The step being waited for says "Chờ …" with a small clock in the info blue ("Chờ Bộ xác nhận"); it never has a dashed edge, which means an unavailable button, and is never a filled pill that looks finished. A failed step is red after a "!" sign, not underlined. Each step starts with a hidden state word for screen readers (Xong:, Hiện tại:, Bước sau:, Lỗi:). Staff screens use pale panels, each a tint and a sign with no edge: `emphasis="subtle"` for Sở đã tiếp nhận, `tone="pending"` for Chờ gửi, `tone="error"` for Bị từ chối, `tone="attention"` for Chưa rõ kết quả. A focused panel title keeps one name: a countdown goes in the text under it. The solid green panel is for the patient's booking confirmation.

## Visual foundations

**Color.** In the clinic theme, FDC blue (`brand-600`, the logo blue) carries the brand. In the nursing home theme the same token is a warm orange, matching fdcmedical.vn, while the logo itself stays blue: primary actions, links and key headings. `brand-700` is the footer and hover state. Light blues (`brand-200`, `brand-300`, `brand-50`) tint sections and tables. Logo green (`leaf-500`) is decorative only. Pink (`accent-600`, `accent-cta`) is reserved for the hotline so it always stands out; do not use it for anything else. Status tags, boxed notices and staff result panels (ConfirmationPanel) are tinted (`status-*-bg`) with a sign and no edge or side bar, so they never look like buttons; no status tag is solid. Red (`status-error`) means something must be fixed; a result outside the normal range is amber (`status-warning`, StatusTag tone `attention`). Every notice, every field error and the save line's failure also show their type as a sign in a circle ("!" error and warning, "i" info, "✓" success), because in the nursing-home theme the orange brand sits close to red and amber.

**Contrast.** Every text role passes 4.5:1 on every surface it may be placed on (`surface`, `surface-alt`, `surface-selected`, the tints and status panels), in both themes. This is checked as a matrix (each role × each allowed surface) on every change, so a pair nobody thought of cannot slip through; ratios are never rounded up (4.498:1 fails). Version 2.2 fixed two pairs the matrix found: `text-link` moved from `brand-600` to `brand-700` (it was 4.498:1 clinic and 4.33:1 nursing home on `surface-alt`), and `leaf-700` darkened to #4f7419 (success text was 4.4:1 on grey and tints). The hotline pink is for white backgrounds only. Placeholders use `text-placeholder` (`ink-soft`), never `ink-subtle`, which is for disabled text only. Five site colors failed and were darkened for the rebuild, keeping their hue: green `leaf-700` (#6ea229 to #577f20, then #4f7419 in 2.2), hotline `accent-600` and `accent-cta`, `ink-subtle` (#999 to #767676), and the nursing home orange `brand-600` (#ec6810 to #bf540d). The bright orange `brand-300` (#ffa234) stays as a band color with dark text only. Version 2 also darkened `danger` (#dc3545 to #b42318, 6.6:1) and added `ink-soft` (#595959) behind `text-muted`, because the old grey fell to 3.8:1 on the `brand-200` section tint. Control borders use `border-control` (#767676, 4.5:1), not the pale `border-input`.

**Type.** Two families, shared by both themes. Headings use **Montserrat** (600–700, line-height 1.3), so the brand still reads as the website. Body text, labels and fields use **Be Vietnam Pro** (400–600), designed by Vietnamese type designers: its stacked tone marks (Ẫ, Ể, Ợ) get about 0.40em of room above capitals versus 0.24em in Montserrat, which matters for older readers. Body is **18px at 1.55** on patient and family screens (`body`); staff tools may use 16px (`body-sm`); nothing a patient must read is smaller than 16px; `caption` (14px) is for metadata only. Use the type style classes (`heading-xl` … `caption`) rather than raw sizes.

**Space and shape.** A 15px gutter grid inside a 1200px container (`container-max`). Spacing steps: 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50px, written in rem so they grow with the reader's text size. Buttons and fields are 48px tall (`control-height`) in the comfortable density and 44px in the compact one; every tap target is at least 44px (`target-min`). Running text stops at about 70 characters (`measure`). Corners are crisp: `radius-sm` (3px) is the default for buttons, inputs and cards. The nursing home's signature is the leaf corner, `radius-leaf` on the top-left and bottom-right only; components apply it automatically to images (`.fdc-media`) and accordion headers in the nursing-home theme. Do not add it elsewhere. Depth is a soft all-around glow (`shadow-card`), not a drop shadow, and only for things that float or one feature card per screen; on dense staff screens separate blocks with background, a 1px `border` edge and spacing instead.

**Accessibility upgrade (version 2, for older patients).** The current site removes focus outlines; this system shows a 3px `focus-ring` outline with a 2px offset on every interactive element; plain links get it from the base styles (use TextLink for links, with `arrow` back, next or none). On an element with a red edge (a field in error, the error summary) the ring is dark ink (`text-default`) with a 2px gap, so in the nursing-home theme orange focus and red error never read as one frame. On top of that: 18px body text, 48px controls and 44px targets, sentence-case labels, Be Vietnam Pro for text, visible labels on every field (placeholders never replace them), errors in words with an error summary that links to each field, typed dates (Ngày / Tháng / Năm) instead of calendar pickers for known dates, phone fields that accept spaces and +84, and the hotline in the same place on every page.

## Iconography

Use the `Icon` atom: 24 single-ink IcoFont glyphs copied from both sites (phone, telephone, calendar, user, pencil, plus/minus, chevrons and more). They inherit the text color; tint them with a role: `text-brand`, `text-muted`, or `hotline` for the hotline only. Size 12–16px inline, 24–40px in cards. SVG copies are in Assets › Icons.

Service illustrations (Assets › Services) are two-tone with FDC blue baked in; use them only in ServiceCard. Specialty icons (Assets › Specialties) are white and sit on a `surface-brand` square in SpecialtyTile, so they follow the theme. Never use emoji as icons.

## Logos

Both logos are in Assets › Logos: the clinic logo (blue mark with green leaf) and the nursing home logo (Điều dưỡng FDC, blue). Use the one that matches the theme, on white or `surface-alt` only; the Header shows it on its white bar. Do not recolor them or place them on `surface-brand`.

## How this system is kept

This design system in Claude Design is the **master**: change tokens, guidelines and wording here. The coded library [`minhhoangico-dot/fdc-ui`](https://github.com/minhhoangico-dot/fdc-ui) mirrors it:

- **Tokens flow from here to code.** After a change here, ask Claude in the fdc-ui project to "sync FDC tokens from Claude Design"; it copies `tokens.json`, regenerates the CSS and re-checks contrast in both themes.
- **Components flow from code to here.** The components' behavior is code, built and tested in fdc-ui, then published into this system as `components/bundle.js`, so these previews run the same code apps use. Ask for component changes in fdc-ui, then "publish FDC components to Claude Design".
- **Each release is checkable.** `release.json` here names the fdc-ui version, the tokens version and the sha256 of every published file; the bundle's second line carries the same stamp. After a publish, Claude reads the files back and compares them with the release; a release counts only when every file matches.
